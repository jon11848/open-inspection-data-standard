# Founding Participant Framework

This page is a discussion draft for organizations considering participation. Listing an organization requires its written permission.

## Founding Member

A Founding Member commits knowledgeable personnel to working-group review, supplies at least one use case or data-model mapping, and supports the project's vendor-neutral objectives.

## Founding Implementer

A Founding Implementer makes the Founding Member commitments and additionally intends to build or demonstrate an import, export, mapping, validator, or API implementation against a published working draft.

## Advisor or Observer

An Advisor or Observer contributes domain, owner, regulatory, professional, research, or standards expertise without an implementation commitment.

## Initial requested commitment

The recommended initial ask is to:

1. review Version 0.1;
2. map relevant platform concepts using `templates/VENDOR_MAPPING.md`;
3. participate in three working sessions;
4. identify one interoperability use case; and
5. permit public identification only after approving the announcement language.

Participation does not constitute product integration, certification, adoption of a final standard, or endorsement of T2D2 or any other participant.
