# Decision 0002: Use the Asset as the enduring organizing entity

- Status: accepted for Version 0.1 working draft
- Date: 2026-07-30

## Decision

The Asset is the enduring subject of inspection data. Projects, facilities, inspection programs, events, findings, issues, and regulatory submissions may change over time, while records remain associated with one or more constructed assets.

The Asset may represent a building, bridge, tunnel, road, utility, tower, industrial facility, transit asset, component, or another constructed asset. Parent-child asset relationships are permitted.
