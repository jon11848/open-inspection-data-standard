# Interim Intellectual Property Policy

> This is an interim project policy, not a substitute for legal advice or a complete standards-body patent policy. It should be reviewed by counsel and replaced or ratified when permanent neutral governance is established.

## Project licensing

Specification prose, diagrams, and documentation are offered under CC BY 4.0. Schemas, API definitions, examples, scripts, and reference code are offered under Apache License 2.0.

Unless a contribution states otherwise and is accepted under a documented exception, contributions are made under the license applicable to the file being modified.

## Contributor representations

By contributing, a contributor represents that:

- the contributor has the right to submit the contribution;
- the contribution is not knowingly subject to an incompatible obligation;
- the contribution does not disclose confidential information without authorization; and
- the contributor agrees to the Developer Certificate of Origin process in `DCO.txt`.

## Patent disclosures

Contributors should disclose any patent claim they know to be essential to implementing a proposed normative requirement and that is owned or controlled by the contributor or the contributor's employer. A disclosure does not itself determine validity, essentiality, infringement, or licensing terms.

No final-standard or certification program should be approved until permanent governance adopts a formal standards intellectual-property and essential-claims policy.

## Trademarks

Product and company names remain the property of their owners. Inclusion in an example or mapping does not imply endorsement. The project's own marks may not be used to imply certification or regulatory approval without authorization.
