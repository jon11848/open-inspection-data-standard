# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participants will maintain a professional, inclusive, technically rigorous environment. Debate ideas and implementation tradeoffs without attacking people or organizations.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Separate verified facts from assumptions.
- Give vendors and practitioners a fair opportunity to explain constraints.
- Protect security-sensitive and personally identifiable information.
- Credit contributions appropriately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, retaliation, or knowingly false claims of certification or endorsement are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

During interim governance, concerns may be reported privately to the repository owner or designated maintainers. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or refer serious matters to appropriate organizational or platform processes. The permanent TSC should publish independent reporting contacts and an appeals process.
