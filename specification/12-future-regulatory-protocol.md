# 12. Future Regulatory Protocol

A future regulator extension may support:

- agency-published filing profiles and machine-readable validation rules;
- professional and firm credential references;
- draft, submission, validation, review, correction, resubmission, acceptance, rejection, extension, and closure states;
- structured regulator comments and responses;
- immutable filing snapshots and audit histories;
- supplemental evidence and unsafe-condition escalation; and
- open submission or approved-provider models selected by each regulator.

The extension should distinguish automated validation from discretionary agency review. Adoption of the OIDS core does not authorize regulatory filing, and each regulator determines applicable law, provider approval, credentials, signatures, records access, and retention.
